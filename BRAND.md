# BRAND.md — Positioning Reference (template)

This file is the source of truth for what your venture stands for. The `scripts/audit-brand.py` script checks it against the 12 canonical sections defined in `agents/brand-auditor.md`. Fill each section in your own words — keep wording terse and specific.

## Mission
*(One sentence, 12–20 words. What you exist to do, in plain language.)*

## Vision
*(One sentence. The state of the world if you succeed.)*

## ICP — Ideal Customer Profile
*(Who you build for, with enough specificity that a stranger could disqualify a lead.)*

## Value proposition
*(One paragraph. What you do for the ICP and why it matters.)*

## Differentiation
*(Three bullets max. What you do that competitors structurally can't.)*

## Pricing
*(Public price + any structured discounts. No "contact us.")*

## Voice
*(Two or three adjectives + one anti-pattern to avoid. Cross-reference SOUL.md for examples.)*

## Off-limits
*(Categories you won't engage. Industry conflicts, ethical lines, capability gaps you won't pretend to have.)*

## Promise
*(What every customer gets, every time. Hard guarantees only — no aspirational language.)*

## Limits
*(What you don't do. Honest disqualifiers protect everyone's time.)*

## Origin
*(Two-sentence story of why this venture exists. Optional but humanizing.)*

## Roadmap signal
*(Public commitment to direction. What's shipping in the next 30 / 90 / 365 days.)*
